# Releasing Bookworms for iPhone

This checklist covers what a TestFlight or App Store build needs beyond a passing local build. Nothing here has been completed for a release yet.

## Before the first TestFlight build

1. **Private Cloud Compute capability.** The Account Holder requests the managed `com.apple.developer.private-cloud-compute` entitlement from **Get the entitlement** on Apple's Private Cloud Compute developer page. Apple grants it to App Store Small Business Program members with fewer than 2 million first-time downloads. Signing a device build with it fails until the provisioning profile includes it. `script/install_on_device.sh` and `script/run_mac.sh` build a test-only variant without it (`NO_PCC`) that designs with the backup model, the on-device model, or local defaults; never archive that variant for TestFlight.
2. **CloudKit production schema.** In CloudKit Console, deploy the `SpineWorklistV1`, `SpineDesignV1`, `CoverChoiceV1`, `SeriesLookV1`, and `SeriesLinkV1` record types, each with a `payload` field of type Bytes, from Development to Production. Without `SpineDesignV1`, every design save fails in Production, and the app stops a run after the first rejected save; without `CoverChoiceV1`, choosing a cover fails with an error; without `SeriesLookV1`, no series look is saved, so each series draws by rank and every reload tries the saves again; without `SeriesLinkV1`, choosing a series or **This book** fails with an error. The app also writes the `LibraryArchive` record type, whose `archive` asset field the Apple TV app's release workflow already requires in Production; confirm it is there, or every library upload fails and each device downloads from Hardcover on its own.
3. **Apple TV build.** Ship an Apple TV build that includes the companion-designs and series-look work, which publishes `SpineWorklistV1`, draws `SpineDesignV1` designs on Book Wall, shows covers chosen in `CoverChoiceV1`, applies `SeriesLinkV1` choices, and creates and draws `SeriesLookV1` looks (see the README's [iCloud contract](../README.md#icloud-contract)). Until then an iPhone without a Hardcover sign-in designs the newest Hardcover snapshot in `library-v1`, and the TV ignores the designs, looks, and choices. Books from the archive of an Apple TV build that does not download `seriesMemberships` group by their featured series. Ship the Apple TV's shared-library work as well, which reads `library-v1` before a due Hardcover fetch, keeps each book's covers fixed by the same rule as this app, and keeps `review`. Released Apple TV builds fetch on their own schedule and drop `review` from every snapshot when they upload, and their covers can differ from this app's after the reader changes an edition, so some designs that a signed-in iPhone makes for its own copy may not match those books on that Apple TV.
4. **Privacy manifest.** Confirm `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` is at the root of the archived `Bookworms.app` and that the archive's privacy report (Organizer, **Generate Privacy Report**) shows only the declared UserDefaults (CA92.1) and file timestamp (C617.1) reasons. The Keychain needs no declared reason.
5. **App Privacy answers.** In App Store Connect, answer the App Privacy questions for the Hardcover sign-in. The app sends the reader's Hardcover tokens only to Hardcover. It reads the reader's own library and block list, and the public profiles, names, photo links, ratings, and finish dates of the readers the account follows or looks up. The library, with the reader's reviews, is cached on the iPhone and saved to the reader's private iCloud database whenever iCloud is available, as the account's snapshot in `library-v1`, where the reader's other Apple devices use it. Signing out deletes the iPhone's copy and leaves the iCloud copy, which the signed-out app still shows. The friends' data (`social-snapshot.json`) stays on the iPhone, and signing out deletes it. **With friends**, **My reading**, and **Share desk** images carry the reader's Hardcover username and profile link only when the reader shares them; **My reading** and **Share desk** images also show it in the preview. The developer can't read the private database, so nothing reaches the developer, as with the Apple TV app's **Data Not Collected** answer in the tvOS release workflow, whose iCloud storage saves the same library there.
6. **Privacy policy.** The privacy policy text in the tvOS release workflow, also published at ian.gay/bookworms-privacy-policy, says that iCloud storage is optional and off by default. Before this app's listing uses that policy, add that the iPhone and Mac app saves the reader's library and reviews to the private iCloud database whenever iCloud is available, and that signing out leaves that copy.

## App Review

Without a Hardcover sign-in, every tab depends on a library that the reviewer's Apple devices saved to their iCloud account: the Apple TV app's upload, or the copy a signed-in iPhone or Mac saves. A reviewer can instead sign in to Hardcover on the iPhone: with no library, the **Share**, **Library**, and **Desk** tabs explain both options and offer **Connect Hardcover**, and the **Spines** tab's account button opens the same sheet. Provide review notes with:

- that the iPhone app reads either the reader's Hardcover library or the library the reader's Apple devices saved to iCloud, and that a signed-in iPhone saves its library there too;
- the Hardcover demo account listed in the tvOS release workflow, and the sign-in steps: **Connect Hardcover**, **Open Hardcover**, sign in on Hardcover's page, enter the code, and allow Bookworms;
- that the Share tab's **With friends** templates compare the reader's ratings with the readers the account follows and with readers looked up by username, so the demo account must follow readers who have rated books, as the tvOS release workflow's App Review sign-in does;
- optional Apple TV setup: install the Apple TV app, connect the same demo account, turn on **iCloud storage** in the Apple TV app's settings, and use the same iCloud account on both devices;
- a demo video of all three tabs with that library;
- how spine generation uses Private Cloud Compute and its per-user daily limit, falls back to Apple's on-device model or to designs made without a model on devices without Private Cloud Compute, uses a third-party backup model only when the reader sets one up, and runs in the background only while charging.

## Signed device checks

Check these on a signed iPhone with the Apple TV on the same iCloud account: the library loads; **Generate now** saves designs and continues after leaving the app; the charging task runs overnight; a worklist push schedules it; the Spines tab names **Private Cloud Compute** and shows the daily limit; and the Apple TV caches the new designs.

Check the model chain on a signed iPhone, following the README's [Spine design model](../README.md#spine-design-model). With Apple Intelligence off and no backup model, the Spines tab names **Local defaults** with why each model is unavailable, and a run designs spines. With a backup model but no key, the tab names the next tier and says the key is missing. With the key, the tab names the backup and why Private Cloud Compute is unavailable, and with a wrong key a run moves to the next tier and shows the provider's message. Later runs start at that tier and show the message beside **Backup model**. **Generate now** tries the backup again, and with the wrong key still saved, the run moves to the next tier at its first book. Replacing the key clears the message, and the next run designs with the backup again. With a custom endpoint whose model takes no images, a run fails three books, then moves to the next tier with the endpoint's message. After Apple Intelligence is back on, the next run gives the books that a weaker tier designed Private Cloud Compute designs, within the daily limit, and their series' unedited looks follow.

Check the Hardcover sign-in on a signed iPhone without an iCloud library, following [Test Hardcover sign-in](../README.md#test-hardcover-sign-in): the code flow completes, the library and its designs load, generation designs the phone's library, **Reconnect** appears after the login is revoked, and **Sign out** removes the iPhone's copy of the library while the Library tab shows the copy in iCloud.

Check the shared library with development-signed builds on one iCloud account, signed in to the same Hardcover account. Tap **Sync now** on the Mac. Then open the app on an iPhone whose copy is at least four hours old, or on a new install, and check that **Last synced** shows the Mac's sync. On an Apple TV build with the shared-library work and **iCloud storage** on, check that the library the Mac synced appears without the Apple TV's own Hardcover fetch while it is less than a day old. A day later, the first device to open the app downloads from Hardcover, and the others adopt its copy.

Check **With friends** on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover, following [Compare with friends](../README.md#compare-with-friends): the followed readers load, best match first; blocked readers never appear; a username and a `hardcover.app/@username` link each look up a reader; Hot Takes, Taste Match, and Book Swap render and share with the profile link; the saved friends' data shows while the iPhone is offline; and a rejected login and **Sign out** each delete the friends' data. Then share **My reading** images: the image letters your profile link; Messages receives each book's `hardcover.app/id/books/` link and the profile link, and both open the right Hardcover pages; the share sheet still offers Instagram (Feed and Stories), **Save Image**, and Messages, and a Story in Instagram shows the link above the reply bar. On the Mac, the share menu still offers **Add to Photos**, which saves the image without the text, and Messages and Mail receive the links.

Check series looks on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover, with a library that has several books of one series and a book in more than one series, such as a Discworld novel, following [Series designs](../README.md#series-designs): after a run, each look group's books draw alike and a `SeriesLookV1` record per group appears in CloudKit Console; a book in several series groups with its shortest one; **Series** lists the book's series with their sizes, and choosing another series or **Not in a series** regroups the book at once and saves a `SeriesLinkV1` record, which choosing **Default** deletes; with **Edit** set to **Series**, a recolor changes every book in the group and the look's record, and **Redesign spines** remakes the look from a new design; with **This book**, the book draws its own design, and a recolor or **Redesign spine** changes only it; a second iPhone on the same iCloud account shows the choices and looks after it reloads; and with an Apple TV build that includes the series-look work, the look whichever device made first draws on both, and the iPhone reuses an Apple TV look without a model request.

Check **Change cover** on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover: the picker lists real edition covers; a choice shows in details, share images, and the Desk scene after it is released; the `CoverChoiceV1` record appears in CloudKit Console; **Redesign spine** saves a design with the new cover's hash; a second iPhone on the same iCloud account shows the choice after it reloads; and **Use default cover** deletes the record.

Check Library's **Stack** with **3D Books** on, on a recent iPhone with the full library, in an optimized build: fling the stack and record its hitches and frame times in Instruments, against the same flings with **3D Books** off, since every visible 3D book corrects its faces for the stack's eye in each frame. Check that the top boards below the middle of the stack and the undersides above it read as one view of the stack, that no board appears late at the screen's top edge or above the caption, and that a book pulled out and put back moves without a jump.

Check the Desk tab's **Desk** scene on a recent iPhone with the full library, in an optimized build: frame rate and memory during and after the drop, shadows, tilt, throwing, and whether the Reduce Motion stacks stay standing after a book is picked up. Also check **Share desk** for **All books**, a year, and a month in each size: the time to the first image, memory while it renders, that the image is not blank, and that the pile returns as you left it when the sheet closes. Check the Share tab's **Desk** template the same way for a lifetime, a year, and a month, with the Desk tab left on the **Desk** scene: switch to Desk while an image renders and back, change options mid-render, and confirm the app does not crash and Desk's pile drops again. Also check what a hidden Desk costs: with the pile dropped, switch to another tab and measure CPU, GPU, and energy in Instruments. Check the power saving too: with Desk showing, turn on Low Power Mode, then, in Xcode's **Devices and Simulators** window, set the device's thermal state condition to **Serious**; each time, confirm that the dust fades out and the soft shadows right under the books go, and that both return when the condition ends. With neither on, and Reduce Motion off, check that warm dust motes show in the lamp's beam while books drop and for a few seconds after, as small, soft, slender flecks, not hard chips, that turn slowly and stay noticeable without drawing the eye from the books, fade out before the desk pauses, so its still holds none, and drift back in without a pop after a tap wakes it. On a Mac, check the same with Low Power Mode. Then measure the pause: with a year's pile dropped and left alone, record 60 seconds of a resting desk in Instruments' Power Profiler, with CPU Profiler, in an optimized build, and again in a build with `BookPileScene.restDelay` raised past the recording, and average a few runs of each. Check that a quick tap on a paused desk lifts its book, that a touch and hold picks one up, that waking shows no flash or color shift, and that the Mac's other animations in the window run at full rate while the desk is paused. With **Tilt** set to **Tip the table**, drop a month's and a year's pile and tip the phone each way: the books pushed against a wall should stay below the navigation bar and inside the screen's sides. In the Mac app's default window and in a wide one, drop a month's pile and check that every book falls onto the table. The Simulator does not report motion, and its physics and rendering timing differ from a device.

Check **Dominoes** on a recent iPhone in an optimized build, from a desk of **All books** and of a month, following [Check the dominoes' setup](../README.md#check-the-dominoes-setup) without its Debug log: the frame rate and hitches while the books set themselves up (Instruments), including at the **Dominoes** press, from five seconds into an **All books** setup, when books held back stop waiting, when the last book lands, and when the lamp's pool settles over the chain 1.5 seconds later, that no book passes through another, and that the setup takes a few seconds at most. Lift a fallen book mid-run and a book of the gathered spread that another lies across, and check that the books resting on it make way without passing through it, both as it leaves and as it comes back, and that the lift starts without a hitch. Then choose **Back to desk** from a standing chain, a fallen one, and the gathered spread, and pick up, carry, and throw a book among the books handed back; with the overview's turned camera as the chain left it, and again after zooming and dragging it, throw books hard toward each edge and check that they come to rest inside the view, below the bar, and clear of the screen's sides; with Reduce Motion on, the books should stand at once.

Run the dominoes on a recent iPhone in an optimized build, for **All books** and a year, with each **Camera**: the chain should fall without pausing from the push to the last book and speed up toward real time over the long chain, never so fast that the books look like toys, the knocks and taps should keep pace with the hits, the Follow camera should keep the falling front near the middle of the view through its swings and push ins and through a switch of **Camera** mid-run, no dust mote should show as a large glowing fleck near the Follow camera, the finale should pull back from the last book before it turns to the gathering books, and the last book should spring up to hang large, lit, and with sharp lettering in front of the still camera, then slam flat into its place without passing through the books under it. Choose another time frame while the dominoes show, once with its covers in the cache and once without, such as after deleting and reinstalling the app: the books should drop into their places without a pause, starting slowly and speeding up as they go.

Check the Desk tab's **Dominoes** recording on a recent iPhone in an optimized build, for **All books** and a month: that the frame rate holds while the run records, from the push through the finale (Instruments' hitches and frame times), and while the chain stands waiting for a push, when the render callback already passes each frame on; how many frames drop when the callback goes on, about a tenth of a second after the chain stands, where the Simulator drops one or two; that the video log's "Recording from a … render in pixel format N" names format 81 (`bgra8Unorm_srgb`), the Simulator's, in which the recording warms Core Image up on a stand-in frame, since a render in another format may compile Core Image's kernels on the render thread in that frame; that a push right after the chain stands still records the run; that the video log's "Recorded N of M frames" shows no dropped frames, also with other apps busy; that lifting a book mid-run keeps the recording, which cuts from the lift to the return while the chain holds still; that **Share** offers **Video** within a few seconds of the heading showing, and that the video's colors, heading, and credit match the screen. Then share the video and the image: **Save Video** and **Save Image** put them in Photos with a "Saved to Photos" toast, and the video posts to an Instagram story and reel and sends in Messages without a format error. Post a [desk video](../README.md#desk-video) from **Share desk** to Instagram too, check that **Save as Live Photo** saves a Live Photo that plays in Photos with a "Saved to Photos" toast, that denying Photos access shows the failure toast, and that the **Desk video** preview shows no credit while the saved and shared files carry it.

Check the Desk on the Mac in an optimized, signed build with a mouse and with a trackpad, following [On the Mac](../README.md#on-the-mac). With the sidebar shown and hidden, and in the smallest window, books come to rest clear of the sidebar and the toolbar. A drag picks a book up at once and throws it when let go, a click opens its details, and an Option-drag turns the view. The pointer shows an open hand over a book and a closed one while it carries one, and moving it over a paused desk leaves the app idle in Activity Monitor. The wheel and two-finger scrolling zoom toward the pointer, scrolling away zooms in with either scrolling direction setting, Shift with the wheel and sideways scrolling turn the view, and scrolls over the sidebar, a sheet, and a book's details still scroll them. A right-click on a book shows its menu, and each item works, including **Change Cover…**; a right-click on the table shows none. After a throw, and after a click, with the pointer held still, a right-click shows no menu and the pointer shows no grab hand until it moves; once the desk rests, both name the book under the pointer again. On the dominoes, the wheel and two-finger scrolling zoom toward the pointer and a drag looks along the path. **View > Zoom In** (Command-Plus; also check whether Command-Equals without Shift zooms in), **Zoom Out**, and **Actual Size**, and **Desk > Set Up Dominoes** and **Back to Desk**, work on the Desk, and **Zoom In** and **Zoom Out** on the dominoes too; with the sidebar shown, they close in on the middle of the visible desk rather than drifting toward the sidebar. They are unavailable in other sections, while a book's details show, and while the **Share desk** sheet or **Change Cover…** shows. The desk still pauses 10 seconds after the last scroll or zoom.

Check the Desk's tips and bar on a recent iPhone and on the Mac, after a fresh install: **Pick up a book** points at a book at the Desk scene's first rest and goes when you close it or pick a book up; **Look around the desk** shows at a rest after two throws; **Push the first book** points at the hand once the chain stands and goes at the push. No tip shows over a moving scene, a tip goes when the desk wakes or when **Dominoes** or **Back to desk** switches the scene, and the desk still pauses with a tip showing. While a tip shows, check whether a touch on the desk, a tap on the hand, and a press of a bar button reach the scene and the bar or only close the tip, and whether a tip closed by a tap outside it stays closed. Pinch or drag the dominoes' camera while the push tip shows: the tip goes and returns at the hand once the camera rests; it also goes while a lifted book's details show. On the Mac, a scroll or a menu zoom takes it down for the rest of that wait. During a dominoes run, the red dot and the time show under the bar and are absent from the video. The dominoes' overview shows covers and spines for a year and for **All books**, and the fallen chain stays inside both screen edges as the camera eases in, for a year and for **All books**. The time frame button fits between the bar's buttons on the narrowest supported iPhone, at the largest standard text size, for a year and for **All books**, shrinking rather than truncating if even its shortest size does not fit; check that a tap just above or below its capsule still opens its menu, or give the button a 44-point target. VoiceOver reads **Dominoes**, **Back to desk**, and the recording badge.

Check the Share tab's **Top Five** in each size on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover: the preview sits just below the navigation bar, **Share** to Instagram posts a Story whose heading clears Instagram's progress bars, whose list titles read comfortably at arm's length, and whose last ranked row, the only content besides the credit in the bottom 250 pixels, stays clear of the reply bar, the cube button beside the size switches between 3D and flat covers and reads as on (tinted, filled cube) or off (outline), a 3D cover looks as bright as its flat cover, VoiceOver reads the button as **3D books** with its state, and choosing **Desk** dims it. Touch and hold the preview: the image fills the screen over both bars while the finger stays down and returns on lift, a scroll that starts on the preview still scrolls the settings, and VoiceOver's **Show full size** action opens the same view, which a double-tap or the escape gesture closes, with VoiceOver back on the preview. On an iPhone without an iCloud library or a Hardcover sign-in, check that the Hardcover sheet opens once at first launch, that the **Share**, **Library**, and **Desk** tabs show **No library yet** with **Connect Hardcover**, that the sheet stays open while the library downloads, and that the tabs show the library once it arrives.
