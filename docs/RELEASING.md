# Releasing Bookworms for iPhone

This checklist covers what a TestFlight or App Store build needs beyond a passing local build. Nothing here has been completed for a release yet.

## Before the first TestFlight build

1. **Private Cloud Compute capability.** The Account Holder requests the managed `com.apple.developer.private-cloud-compute` entitlement from **Get the entitlement** on Apple's Private Cloud Compute developer page. Apple grants it to App Store Small Business Program members with fewer than 2 million first-time downloads. Signing a device build with it fails until the provisioning profile includes it. `script/install_on_device.sh` builds a test-only variant without it (`NO_PCC`); never archive that variant for TestFlight.
2. **CloudKit production schema.** In CloudKit Console, deploy the `SpineWorklistV1`, `SpineDesignV1`, and `CoverChoiceV1` record types, each with a `payload` field of type Bytes, from Development to Production. Without `SpineDesignV1`, every design save fails in Production, and the app stops a run after the first rejected save; without `CoverChoiceV1`, choosing a cover fails with an error.
3. **Apple TV build.** Merge and ship the tvOS `spine-companion` work, which publishes `SpineWorklistV1`, draws `SpineDesignV1` designs on Book Wall, and shows covers chosen in `CoverChoiceV1` (see the README's [iCloud contract](../README.md#icloud-contract)). Until then an iPhone without a Hardcover sign-in designs the newest Hardcover snapshot in `library-v1`, and the TV ignores the designs and cover choices.
4. **Mac companion build.** Push and ship a Mac companion build that includes macos commit `f2eed75`, which reads `CoverChoiceV1` and keeps current iPhone designs. Older Mac builds replace iPhone designs whenever they recheck a book, so the two companions keep overwriting each other's designs and spend model requests.
5. **Privacy manifest.** Confirm `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` is at the root of the archived `Bookworms.app` and that the archive's privacy report (Organizer, **Generate Privacy Report**) shows only the declared UserDefaults (CA92.1) and file timestamp (C617.1) reasons. The Keychain needs no declared reason.
6. **App Privacy answers.** In App Store Connect, answer the App Privacy questions for the Hardcover sign-in: the app sends the reader's Hardcover token only to Hardcover to read their own library, and keeps the library and reviews on the iPhone.

## App Review

Without a Hardcover sign-in, every tab depends on the library that the Apple TV app uploads to the reviewer's iCloud account. A reviewer can instead sign in to Hardcover on the iPhone: with no library, the **Share**, **Library**, and **Desk** tabs explain both options and offer **Connect Hardcover**, and the **Spines** tab's account button opens the same sheet. Provide review notes with:

- that the iPhone app reads either the reader's Hardcover library or the Apple TV app's iCloud library;
- the Hardcover demo account listed in the tvOS release workflow, and the sign-in steps: **Connect Hardcover**, **Open Hardcover**, sign in on Hardcover's page, enter the code, and allow Bookworms;
- optional Apple TV setup: install the Apple TV app, connect the same demo account, turn on **iCloud storage** in the Apple TV app's settings, and use the same iCloud account on both devices;
- a demo video of all three tabs with that library;
- how spine generation uses Private Cloud Compute and its per-user daily limit, and that background generation runs only while charging.

## Signed device checks

Check these on a signed iPhone with the Apple TV on the same iCloud account: the library loads; **Generate now** saves designs and continues after leaving the app; the charging task runs overnight; a worklist push schedules it; the Spines tab shows the daily limit; and the Apple TV caches the new designs.

Check the Hardcover sign-in on a signed iPhone without an Apple TV library, following [Test Hardcover sign-in](../README.md#test-hardcover-sign-in): the code flow completes, the library and its designs load, generation designs the phone's library, **Reconnect** appears after the login is revoked, and **Sign out** removes the library.

Check **Change cover** on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover: the picker lists real edition covers; a choice shows in details, share images, and the Desk scene after it is released; the `CoverChoiceV1` record appears in CloudKit Console; **Redesign spine** saves a design with the new cover's hash; a second iPhone on the same iCloud account shows the choice after it reloads; and **Use default cover** deletes the record.

Check the Desk tab's **Desk** scene on a recent iPhone with the full library, in an optimized build: frame rate and memory during and after the drop, shadows, tilt, throwing, and whether the Reduce Motion stacks stay standing after a book is picked up. Also check **Share desk** for **All books**, a year, and a month in each size: the time to the first image, memory while it renders, that the image is not blank, and that the pile returns as you left it when the sheet closes. Check the Share tab's **Desk** template the same way for a lifetime, a year, and a month, with the Desk tab left on the **Desk** scene: switch to Desk while an image renders and back, change options mid-render, and confirm the app does not crash and Desk's pile drops again. Also check what a hidden Desk costs: with the pile dropped, switch to another tab and measure CPU, GPU, and energy in Instruments. The Simulator does not report motion, and its physics and rendering timing differ from a device.

Check **Set up dominoes** on a recent iPhone in an optimized build, from a desk of **All books** and of a month, following [Check the dominoes' setup](../README.md#check-the-dominoes-setup) without its Debug log: the frame rate and hitches while the books set themselves up (Instruments), including at the menu choice, from five seconds into an **All books** setup, when books held back stop waiting, when the last book lands, and when the lamp's pool settles over the chain 1.5 seconds later, that no book passes through another, and that the setup takes a few seconds at most. Lift a fallen book mid-run and a book of the gathered spread that another lies across, and check that the books resting on it make way without passing through it, both as it leaves and as it comes back, and that the lift starts without a hitch. Then choose **Back to desk** from a standing chain, a fallen one, and the gathered spread, and pick up, carry, and throw a book among the books handed back; with Reduce Motion on, the books should stand at once.

Check the Desk tab's **Dominoes** recording on a recent iPhone in an optimized build, for **All books** and a month: that the frame rate holds while the run records, from the push through the finale (Instruments' hitches and frame times), and while the chain stands waiting for a push, when the render callback already passes each frame on; how many frames drop when the callback goes on, about a tenth of a second after the chain stands, where the Simulator drops one or two; that the video log's "Recording from a … render in pixel format N" names format 81 (`bgra8Unorm_srgb`), the Simulator's, in which the recording warms Core Image up on a stand-in frame, since a render in another format may compile Core Image's kernels on the render thread in that frame; that a push right after the chain stands still records the run; that the video log's "Recorded N of M frames" shows no dropped frames, also with other apps busy; that lifting a book mid-run keeps the recording, which cuts from the lift to the return while the chain holds still; that **Share** offers **Video** within a few seconds of the heading showing, and that the video's colors, heading, and credit match the screen. Then share the video and the image: **Save Video** and **Save Image** put them in Photos with a "Saved to Photos" toast, and the video posts to an Instagram story and reel and sends in Messages without a format error. Post a [desk video](../README.md#desk-video) from **Share desk** to Instagram too, check that **Save as Live Photo** saves a Live Photo that plays in Photos with a "Saved to Photos" toast, that denying Photos access shows the failure toast, and that the **Desk video** preview shows no credit while the saved and shared files carry it.

Check the Share tab's **Year Stack** for **Lifetime** in each size on a signed iPhone that is signed in to Hardcover, after a sync that downloads series names: series show as one tied bundle in one cloth with their name on a paper label and their count on its tag, including a series whose books have spine designs in different colors, the preview swipes through every page, **Share** to Instagram offers the pages as one carousel post or a run of stories in order, a posted Story page shows its heading clear of Instagram's progress bars and profile row and, on a current iPhone, its shelf, page label, and credit clear of the reply bar, the share sheet's **Save 3 Images** (for three pages) puts every page in Photos in order, and the app stays responsive with reasonable memory while many pages render.

On an iPhone without an Apple TV library or a Hardcover sign-in, check that the Hardcover sheet opens once at first launch, that the **Share**, **Library**, and **Desk** tabs show **No library yet** with **Connect Hardcover**, that the sheet stays open while the library downloads, and that the tabs show the library once it arrives.
